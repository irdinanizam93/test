Let's Explore Your Dream Destination Here!

Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque

City Center

Standing 52 meters tall, it was designed as the tallest building in central Bandar Seri Begawan; local lore notes that nearby commercial buildings were restricted from exceeding its height.

Jerudong Park

Brunei-Muara

Jerudong Park is an amusement park in Jerudong, Brunei-Muara District, Brunei. It is the largest and most expensive amusement park in Southeast Asia, built and funded by the Brunei Government for $500 billion.

Brunei Energy Hub Dermaga Diraja

City Center

Brunei Energy Hub Dermaga Diraja stands as a significant tribute to the country's history and serves as an interactive educational centre for the public to learn about the importance of the energy industry to the local economy as well as everyday lives

Kampong Ayer

Kampong Ayer

Kampong Ayer is the world's largest stilt settlement, located on the Brunei River in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei. Often called the "Venice of the East," it has been inhabited for over 600 to 1,000 years.

Ulu Temburong National Park

Temburong

Ulu Temburong National Park is the first national park to be established in Brunei, protected since 1991. The park is in Temburong District in eastern Brunei, and covers about 40% of the district in the south at 550 square kilometres.

Royal Regalia Museum

Bandar Seri Begawan

The Royal Regalia Museum in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei, is a grand, golden-domed landmark dedicated to the history of the Bruneian monarchy and the reign of Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah.
