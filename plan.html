Practical Travel Information

Essential details on getting around, entry requirements, and accommodation.

🛂 Visas & Entry

Entry Requirements

Most visitors enjoy visa-free entry for up to 14 or 30 days. Ensure your passport has at least six (6) months of validity remaining.

🚗 Transportation

Getting Around

Taxis and ride-hailing apps like Dart are available in Bandar Seri Begawan. Renting a car or booking guided tours is highly recommended for better experience.

🏨 Stays

Accommodation

Brunei offers the perfect stay for you ranging from luxurious hotels to budget-friendly homestays.

Local Cuisine & Dining

Taste the unique flavors of Bruneian heritage.

National Dish

Ambuyat Experience

Made from the inner trunk of the sago palm, ambuyat is a starchy delicacy served with savory sour-spicy fruit gravies (cacah) and local sides.

Night Markets

Gadong Night Market

The ultimate destination for street food lovers. Sample local cuisine for very affordable prices.

Popular Local Eats

Nasi Katok

Brunei's iconic meal consisting of white rice, fried chicken, and sambal.

Culture, Heritage & Customs

Respectful travel tips and local etiquette.

👔 Dress Etiquette

Dress Code & Greetings

Dress modestly when visiting mosques. A light handshake is common, though traditional greetings involve a gentle touch of the hand to the chest.

🕌 Traditions & Customs

Islamic Heritage

As an Islamic country, alcohol is not sold publicly and weekend closures occur briefly on Fridays for Friday prayers.

🫱🏼‍🫲🏽 Social Etiquette

General Courtesy

Avoid public displays of affection (PDA) like kissing. This can lead to legal penalties under local laws.
